=== SSwurve-Style Viral Clip Deck ===

Drop your pre-rendered AI video clips (.mp4) in this folder.
They will automatically appear on your Dashboard under the "Viral Clip Deck" section!

How to create photorealistic AI clips like SSwurve:
1. Record a 5-10 second clip of yourself sitting in your gaming chair looking straight at the camera.
2. Upload the clip or a clear photo of yourself to:
   - Kling AI (https://klingai.com) -> Video-to-Video mode
   - Runway Gen-3 (https://runwayml.com) -> Video-to-Video mode
   - Luma Dream Machine (https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine)
   - Viggle AI (https://viggle.ai) for dance and character swaps
3. Use high-impact prompts:
   - "Streamer transforms into Jay-Z in black tuxedo and bow tie, live studio lighting, 4K"
   - "Massive realistic fire-breathing dragon roaring behind streamer in gaming bedroom, fiery embers, smoke"
   - "Streamer transforms into an ancient nun in black habit smoking a cigarette, rising smoke, hyper-realistic"
   - "Streamer flying over Mount Fuji at supersonic speed, dynamic speed lines, cinematic anime"
4. Download the generated .mp4 and save it in this 'media_clips' folder (e.g., 'dragon.mp4', 'jayz.mp4', 'nun.mp4').
5. Open your Dashboard (http://localhost:8000/dashboard) and click the clip button or press hotkeys to trigger it live!
